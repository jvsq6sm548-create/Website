Underwater

You may not expect much from diving in the Lake Constance, but if you look closly enought you will often find beautiful subjects like this quagga mussel (Dreissena bugensis) on a shipwreck. Also if you are calm and patient, you may even encounter a northern pike (Esox lucius). But they are often gone as soon as you exhale and bubbles escape from your mouth.
